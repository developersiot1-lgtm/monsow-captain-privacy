Monsow Captain Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026
Publisher: Monsow POS
Support: aarthiravi63@gmail.com

Purpose and information handled

Monsow Captain connects authorised restaurant staff to an existing MONSOW POS server. The app handles the selected server address, account identity and login session. Based on staff permissions and restaurant workflow, the connected server may provide table, menu, order, kitchen-ticket, sales, tax, payment, customer-contact and note information.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to connect to the selected restaurant server, authenticate the account, show permitted ordering information and submit orders or kitchen tickets. Data is transmitted to the selected server using its configured secure connection. The restaurant or company operating that server controls its accounts and business records. Hosting, tunnel and security providers used by that company may process connection and security logs to operate and protect the service. Monsow Captain does not sell user data and does not include advertising.

Information stored on the phone

The app may save selected server links, browser storage and a valid encrypted login session so it can reconnect. Entered passwords are not saved by the native launcher. Saved sessions are protected using Android Keystore-based encryption where supported. Removing a connection, using the clear-local-data control, clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data. These actions do not delete restaurant records held by the company server.

QR scanning and permissions

The app can use Google Play services to scan a Captain pairing QR code. The app receives decoded connection text and does not keep a camera photo from the scan. A manual server-address option is available. The app does not request broad photo/storage, contacts, SMS, microphone or location permissions.

Retention and deletion requests

Local links and sessions remain until removed, cleared, expire or the app is uninstalled. Restaurant server records and security logs are retained by the restaurant, its hosting providers and applicable record-keeping requirements. To request access, correction, account revocation or deletion of server-held data, contact your restaurant administrator and aarthiravi63@gmail.com. Identity verification and legal retention obligations may apply.

Security

The app is designed to use secure server connections and certificate validation. Access is controlled by account permissions configured by the restaurant. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children and policy changes

Monsow Captain is intended for restaurant business staff and is not directed to children. We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact
